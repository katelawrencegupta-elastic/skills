# Canvas and PDF layout

Open this only when changing a canvas or `render_pdf.py`. The two stay in the same order. PDFs are not screenshots of the canvas.

PDFs are rendered by `render_pdf.py` from `account.json` (inline SVG, Letter landscape, Chrome `--print-to-pdf`; `CHROME_PATH` overrides).

Account section order starts with the snapshot and the Current ECH architecture table on the same page when the tallest region has at most 12 deployments. The table lists each deployment with its Elastic version, region, classified solution, availability zones, lookback ECU, utilization (share of lookback ECU), stored size, document count, and average document size. A single-zone row stays highlighted. The topology is the next page: a diagram of provider, region, and availability-zone groups, then each component as RAM × node count. That count is the zone count on the Cloud SKU: ECH places one node in each zone, and extra autoscaled nodes are not a separate field. A taller fleet keeps the architecture table on its own page and shows the first 12 deployments in each region on the topology page. Then 1 platform (zone, billing, and instance tables in shaded boxes, with space between the boxes), 2 versions & ECU, **3 burndown and 4 deployments over time on one page** (each chart and the balance table in its own panel; the balance panel adds the 3- and 6-month projection at the trailing-year complete-month pace), **5 storage on its own page** (stored GB over time) and **6 ECU by solution on its own page** (monthly ECU as a percent of the commit), **7 ML on its own page**, and 8 Current State Analysis & Recommendations on its own page with the action, the impact, and the Elastic docs that apply. **§9 cost recs and §10 sizing move onto the consolidation-savings PDF** when that report runs (ECH > 50 %); they stay on the account PDF otherwise. A deployment billed in 1 availability zone raises a warning on §1 and a performance recommendation. Long display tables are capped (top 4–6). `account.json` keeps the full cloud, storage, and AI deployment lists.

## §11 Serverless consolidation savings

This section belongs to `diag-serverless-consolidation`. The account-summary skill does not build it.

`<slug>-serverless-consolidation-savings.canvas.tsx` is the quoted consolidation. By default that is bulk ingest and measured retention from the diagnostic bundles (`brief.ingest_source` = `diagnostics`). The diagnostic run writes two PDFs from that bulk: `<key>_Serverless_consolidation_savings` at the measured month rounded up, and `<key>_Serverless_consolidation_30d` at month 1. Each links to its own sheet. `<slug>-serverless-consolidation-from-k360.canvas.tsx` exists only when `brief.k360_ingest` is set: stored GB ÷ assumed retention. K360 does not provide a raw ingest total, so that backup can sit far below measured bulk. Build each from its own brief block (`savings` / `quotes` / `notes`, or `k360_ingest.savings` / `k360_ingest.quotes` / `k360_ingest.notes`). Do not mix the quotes. The backup PDF is `<key>_Serverless_consolidation_from_k360.pdf`. The backup does not get the 30-day pass.

Own canvas (`<slug>-serverless-consolidation-savings.canvas.tsx`), ECH > 50 % only, built from `*_brief.json`.

When a contract rate exists, headline Stats compare ECH at ARR ÷ (commit ECU/day × 365) with serverless at estimator list. The $1/ECU list view is omitted. With no ARR or commit, the comparison stays at $1/ECU. The savings ECU/mo is the complete-month average. The deployment table divides lookback ECU by lookback days ÷ 30.416. Those two figures do not add up. A single-region year can be the sheet's year-1 cell. The savings annual is 12 × the monthly read-back. Then data used and the arithmetic → 1 provisioned→metered → 2 fixed overhead→zero → 3 one serverless project per solution in each region → 4 per-deployment table (ECU, and contract $ = ECU × that rate when a rate exists) → 5 stored GB ÷ months, which is not the bulk rate and does not reprice a diagnostic quote, plus fleet at the contract rate and the scaled serverless estimate → 6 the read-backs that add up to the serverless total → 9 cost recs → 10 sizing. Page 1 can show Observability deployment-count bars. The 100% bar reuses the headline Observability total. Search-power profiles are charted and are not added to D32 or E74. Quote D32 only when unformatted G26 is within 0.51 GB/day of the bulk written. Quote E74 only when E73 is 0.

The component page stacks the By component table and the Sizing inputs table at full width. Sizing inputs keeps Solution, Deployments, ECU/mo, Stored GB, Kept (mo), and GB/day on one row; do not put that table in a side column. Section 1 puts the ECH-versus-serverless comparison and the monthly build-up chart in two side-by-side panels. The projects table is a full-width row under that pair, not inside the comparison panel. Section 4 shades each shown deployment whose fixed overhead is above 50% of its ECU and captions the shading.

When a solution spans regions, each project gets its own sizing named by region, and the report links each one. The headline is the sum of those read-backs. Search $ is the es3-estimator on-demand read-back for that region. When Search deployments bill jina embeddings, the ML-node ECU on those deployments is added to the EIS line at list, along with the billed inference tokens. ML nodes on deployments with no jina SKU stay out of that line. Observability and Security $ are the sheet read-backs. The contract rate is applied to ECH only. Observability and Security tokens stay on the §7 meter and are not added to Search.
